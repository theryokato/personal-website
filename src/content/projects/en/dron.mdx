## Designing for the fireground
DRON is a proof of concept for gathering information at structural fires. The team is developing a UAV that first responders could deploy quickly to locate hotspots and map their surroundings.

### A serviceable airframe
As mechanical systems lead, my focus is the airframe. The arms carry propeller torque, while the central structure supports the payload. Weight, stiffness and access for repairs all constrain the design.

For V4, the team selected carbon-fiber arm tubes to limit vibration. Screws and heat-set inserts let the assembly come apart for repairs. The CAD view shows the arm interfaces around the central payload enclosure.

### Integrating sensing and autonomy
The vision team calibrated two stereo cameras and published their images through ROS 2 for visualization in Unity. The autonomy team demonstrated multiple simulated vehicles in Gazebo using PX4, with sensor output available in ROS 2.

### Flight evidence
The video records a prototype flight test. The electrical design uses a 3000 mAh battery. The project's estimates are approximately 12 minutes of maneuvering flight and 15 minutes at low speed or hover. Measured endurance and vibration data are still needed to evaluate the V4 design.
